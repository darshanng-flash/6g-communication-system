Understanding Attack Types
Normal

Regular network traffic with expected behavior patterns. No security threat detected.

Flooding Attack

Unusually high number of control packets causing network congestion. Can lead to Denial of Service.

Blackhole Attack

Node drops all packets causing complete data loss. Critical threat to network integrity.

Grayhole Attack

Selective packet dropping with inconsistent reception pattern. Harder to detect than blackhole.
